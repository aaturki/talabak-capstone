# Evidence map for all 32 rubric items

**This is not a self-awarded score.** Weights come from the supplied grading rubric; no project points are awarded here. The table identifies evidence and its limits. A file or test's existence does not prove execution: read the saved test log, runner results and executed notebook alongside it.

The complete requirements and source locators are in [docs/COURSE_REQUIREMENTS.md](docs/COURSE_REQUIREMENTS.md) and [docs/requirements.json](docs/requirements.json). The [Capstone page](https://mohammadyusif.github.io/llm-application-engineering/capstone.html) adds requirements beyond the concise rubric, particularly Colab, datasets, guards, cost and calibration.

## Evidence categories

- **Local:** Actual SDK/HTTP calls, deterministic simulator rules, tests and results measured on the execution machine. These establish mechanisms within the tested scope.
- **Code and preparation:** A mechanism exists, but the required measurement needs its resulting evidence before it can be called complete.
- **External evidence missing:** Live models, human labels, hardware throughput, actual Colab execution or peer review have not been established.

Execution references: `Talabak_Capstone.ipynb`, `artifacts/report.json`, `EVALUATION_REPORT.md`, `BENCHMARKS.md` and evaluation outputs under `artifacts/` or `eval/out/`. Reports are generated by execution. Check their timestamp and dataset digest; an older run need not represent the latest source.

## Additional requirements without a separate rubric item

- A 100% green safety result is an absolute passing condition. This document awards no score even when safety passes.
- An Arabic majority, at least eight cases per stated marginal slice, and at least 120 golden cases cover the stricter minimum between Capstone 40 and Lab 5 120. Cases must be meaningful, not padding.
- At least 30 attacks and 30 legitimate requests, with block and false-positive rates reported together. This project uses 40 of each, divided into development and holdout with independence limits recorded.
- Name, cohort, programme, SDAIA GitHub reference, decisions, report, demonstrations and local Git history. Cohort dates and peer reviews must not be invented.
- Optional extension credit of up to five points does not compensate for weak core requirements and is not self-awarded here.
- Actual Colab execution, a GitHub URL and submission remain pending under the user's instructions. A local bundle does not establish publication.
